# VIDEO BACKGROUND SETUP INSTRUCTIONS

## Your homepage hero section is now ready for a background video!

### 📁 Where to Place Your Video Files

Put your video file(s) in this `/videos/` folder with the following name(s):

**Primary video file:**
- `hero-background.mp4` (recommended - best browser support)

**Optional additional format for better compatibility:**
- `hero-background.webm` (WebM format for modern browsers)

---

## 🎥 Video Specifications

### Recommended Settings:
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 1280x720 (HD)
- **Duration:** 10-30 seconds (will loop automatically)
- **File Size:** Under 10MB for fast loading
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (widescreen)
- **Frame Rate:** 24-30 fps

### Content Tips:
- Action shots, interviews, speaking moments
- Not too much motion (can be distracting)
- Good lighting
- Professional quality

---
