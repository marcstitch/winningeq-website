Full Biography

Complete background for in-depth features and profiles

Marc Stcherbina is a keynote speaker and professional interviewer who has spent over 15 years gaining unprecedented access to the world's most successful athletes, coaches, and leaders. Through thousands of interviews conducted for Fox Sports, ESPN, BBC Sport, CBS Sports, ITV, and ABC Australia, Marc has explored what separates champions from everyone else—and discovered that the principles of elite performance translate powerfully from sports to business.

Broadcasting Background
Marc's broadcasting career has included coverage of NBA Finals, Super Bowls, World Championships, and Olympic Games. He has interviewed NBA champions, Olympic medalists, Super Bowl-winning coaches, Formula 1 team principals, and business leaders who've built billion-dollar sports franchises. This extensive experience has given Marc unique insights into leadership under pressure, team dynamics, resilience, and the psychology of sustained excellence.

Speaking & Corporate Work
Recognizing the universal leadership lessons emerging from his sports interviews, Marc began translating these insights for corporate audiences in 2018. His keynote presentations don't rely on theory or business case studies—they're built on actual conversations with proven winners who've performed at the highest levels.

Marc's four signature keynotes address the challenges facing modern leaders: making decisions under pressure, developing practical emotional intelligence, building championship cultures, and maintaining resilience through adversity. Each presentation is customized to the audience and objectives, combining compelling stories with actionable frameworks that drive real behavior change.

Interview & Moderation Services
Beyond keynote speaking, Marc offers professional interview and moderation services that bring broadcast-quality expertise to corporate events. Whether conducting one-on-one interviews with executives, moderating expert panels, leading fireside chats, or recording live podcasts, Marc's thorough preparation and natural delivery style consistently bring out the best insights from participants.

What Makes Marc Different
Marc's credibility comes from real access to real champions. Every insight in his presentations comes from someone who's actually done it at the highest level—NBA champions who've won in Game 7, coaches who've built dynasty teams, athletes who've bounced back from devastating losses. This authenticity, combined with Marc's broadcast experience and natural storytelling ability, creates presentations that are both entertaining and transformative.

Client Impact
Marc's clients include Fortune 500 companies, technology startups, professional associations, and industry conferences. Organizations bring Marc in when they need practical insights on leadership, performance, and culture—delivered by someone whose daily work involves studying excellence at the highest levels.
