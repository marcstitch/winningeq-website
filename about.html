About Marc Stcherbina

Keynote Speaker | Professional Interviewer | Leadership Expert

The Day That Changed Everything

December 6th, 2008

In a split second, everything changed. A routine rugby tackle resulted in a broken neck—fractured vertebrae that could have ended not just my career, but my life.

Lying on that field, unable to move, surrounded by paramedics, I faced a terrifying reality: I didn't know if I'd ever walk again. The injury forced me to confront what mattered most and gave me something I never expected—a second chance at life.

That moment became the foundation of everything I do today. It taught me that life is too precious to show up half-heartedly, too short to tolerate work that drains us, and too valuable to waste on teams and cultures that don't bring out our best.

This is my 'why': I help people show up fully—for themselves, for each other, and for the work that matters—because I know firsthand how fragile and precious every moment truly is. When we create environments where people thrive, where they feel valued and engaged, we're not just building better organizations. We're honoring the gift of life itself.

From Game Time To Showtime

During his decorated professional rugby career, Marc performed on some of the world's biggest sporting stages in front of tens of thousands of fans. He translated that experience of performing under pressure into a 26-year broadcasting career with major global media outlets.

Influenced by high-performance environments and world-class leaders, Marc transitioned into leadership coaching, alongside his broadcasting and tv hosting work. Through interviews, coaching sessions, and workshops, he identified universal patterns in how elite leaders and organizations build winning cultures.

Today, Marc delivers the insights from these three realms through impactful keynote presentations and professional interview services that help leaders manage pressure, while helping organizations sustain excellence to perform at their best.

Sports Broadcasting Career

Marc's broadcasting career includes work with Fox Sports, ESPN, BBC Sport, CBS Sports, ITV, and ABC Australia. He has covered International rugby matches including European Autumn International series, Pacific Nations Cup in North America and Super Rugby Americas. His domestic professional league experience includes Sydney club rugby, UK & Irish Celtic League, and most recently 8 seasons of Major League Rugby in the USA, interviewing the biggest names in sports, while gaining unprecedented access to championship teams and elite performers.

Professional Rugby Playing Career

Marc's professional rugby career spanned over a decade across elite competitions in Australia, Europe, and international sevens. He rose through Australia's representative pathways, captaining Australian Schoolboys on multiple occasions before representing Australia A and the national sevens team. Marc played Super Rugby with the NSW Waratahs before competing in top-tier European leagues with clubs including Biarritz, Northampton Saints, Cardiff Blues, and Newport Gwent Dragons. His career included more than 160 professional appearances and a Commonwealth Games bronze medal, forged in high-pressure, high-performance environments.

Marc's playing experience includes:

  • ⬭ Leadership roles from national age-group level through senior professional rugby
  • ⬭ Competing in Super Rugby, European club competitions, and international sevens
  • ⬭ Performing under pressure across diverse rugby cultures and team systems
  • ⬭ Representing Australia on the international stage, including Commonwealth Games competition
