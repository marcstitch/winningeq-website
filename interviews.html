Four Interview Formats

Tailored to your event and objectives

1

Virtual Interview

20-45 minutes

Record an interview on a topic of your choice for promotion and marketing purposes eg. LinkedIn Live or podcast style recording.

Best For:

  • Internal newsletters
  • External marketing content
  • Thought leadership series
  • Company website content

What's Included:

  • Custom question development
  • Introduction & outro scripting
  • Live audience engagement if requested
  • Post-production assets
2

Fireside Chat

30-60 minutes

Intimate, conversational format that creates a relaxed atmosphere for deeper storytelling and personal insights. Perfect for executive interviews, founder stories, or intimate gatherings.

Best For:

  • Executive dinners
  • VIP receptions
  • Board retreats
  • Intimate events (50-200 people)

What's Included:

  • Extended pre-conversation with guest
  • Flexible, organic discussion flow
  • Personal storytelling focus
  • Audience interaction opportunities
3

Panel Moderation

45-75 minutes

Expert moderation that ensures all panelists contribute, discussions stay on track, and audiences get valuable insights. Marc balances multiple voices while maintaining energy and engagement.

Best For:

  • Conference breakout sessions
  • Industry roundtables
  • Leadership panels
  • Expert discussions

What's Included:

  • Pre-panel calls with all participants
  • Structured discussion framework
  • Audience Q&A management
  • Time management & flow control
4

One-on-One Interview

20-45 minutes

Classic interview format perfect for keynote speakers, executives, or special guests. Marc's preparation and conversational style brings out the best insights and stories from your featured guest.

Best For:

  • Conference keynote follow-ups
  • Executive conversations
  • Podcast episodes
  • Corporate town halls

What's Included:

  • Pre-interview research & preparation
  • Custom question development
  • Professional on-stage delivery
  • Post-event content recommendations
